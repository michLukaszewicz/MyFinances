## Notes

Dashboard donut charts (spend and income) are currently hardcoded to the current month. Let the user choose the period they show. Applies to both existing charts. Options: "last 30 days", "last 90 days", "selected month", and a custom date range. Replaces S-05 (budget-vs-actual), dropped from the MVP on 2026-10-02 in favour of better data exploration. Related: parked FR-016 (arbitrary date-range filter) in the roadmap.
